https://cogni.cards/anatomy/ATTRIBUTION.md
Cogni Anatomy rebuilt axes from landmarks, scaled and placed the geometry,
simplified it and recomputed normals. This app selects a subset, adjusts the
viewing scale and position, and supplies its own colours and learning interface.
The colouring and sequence highlights are explanatory, not real tissue colours
or measured motion. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.
